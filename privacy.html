Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

BeSovereign (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the website besovereign.org. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit the site. We are committed to keeping the site simple and respectful of your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

BeSovereign does not require user accounts or logins. We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, or physical address.

However, like most websites, certain technical information may be processed automatically:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • IP address (typically handled by the hosting provider)
  • Pages visited and approximate time spent on the site
  • Referral source (how you arrived at the site)

Your preferred order of the data cards on the homepage is stored only in your own browser’s local storage. This information never leaves your device.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies or similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential / Functional — to remember settings such as the order of dashboard cards.
  • Analytics — to understand general usage patterns and improve the site (if enabled).
  • Advertising — if we display ads through Google AdSense or other advertising partners, those partners may set cookies to serve relevant advertisements and measure their performance.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some site features.

4. Advertising

We may display advertisements on the site through third-party networks, including Google AdSense and/or crypto-focused advertising networks.

These partners may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide relevant ads. This may include approximate location (based on IP address), browser information, and browsing behaviour.

Google’s use of advertising cookies is covered by Google’s own privacy policies. You can learn more and manage ad personalisation at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ad Settings

We do not control the cookies or data practices of third-party advertisers.

5. Third-Party Services

The site displays data from third-party sources (for example Mempool.space, CoinGecko, exchange APIs, and TradingView). These services have their own privacy policies. We do not share any personal information with them beyond what is necessary for the public APIs to function.

The site is hosted via GitHub Pages. GitHub’s privacy practices apply to the hosting infrastructure.

6. How We Use Information

Any technical information collected is used only to:

  • Operate and maintain the website
  • Understand general usage so we can improve the experience
  • Display advertisements (if enabled)
  • Protect the site against abuse or technical problems

We do not sell personal information.

7. Data Retention

Because we do not maintain user accounts, we do not store personal profiles. Technical logs held by the hosting provider or analytics tools are retained according to those providers’ policies, typically for a limited period.

8. Your Choices

You can:

  • Clear or block cookies in your browser
  • Use browser private/incognito mode
  • Opt out of personalised Google ads via Google’s Ad Settings
  • Contact us if you have questions about this policy

9. Children’s Privacy

The site is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates the latest revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via the contact methods provided on the site or through the public GitHub repository associated with BeSovereign.
